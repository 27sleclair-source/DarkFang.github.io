Welcome to the World of Wolves

Wolves are one of North America's most important predators. They help keep ecosystems balanced, live in complex family groups called packs, and have played an important role in the history and culture of the continent.

🐾 Species

North America is home to Gray Wolves, Red Wolves, and Arctic Wolves.

🏔 Habitats

Wolves can be found in forests, mountains, tundras, and grasslands.

🌎 Ecosystems

Wolves help maintain healthy ecosystems by controlling prey populations.

About This Project

This website explores the history of wolves in North America, their role in nature, and conservation efforts that help protect wolf populations today.
